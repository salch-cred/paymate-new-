# Brag Plan: PayMate

## What is this app?
PayMate is an on-chain invoicing and settlement tool for independent workers to create intelligent invoices and collect direct USDC payments via GOAT Network.

## The angle
A sleek, fast-paced product launch that highlights the real-world utility: you do the work, you get paid in USDC, and you get a verified reputation. It contrasts the slow "admin spiral" of traditional invoices with the instant, verified nature of x402 settlement.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The phrase "Get paid. Keep the proof." types out character-by-character over the dark, premium gradient background.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The natural language prompt ("Brand strategy and launch system...") generating a pristine 3-line invoice instantly.
- The invoice preview card sliding into view.
- The live settlement trace verifying the payment on GOAT Network.

## Outro / punchline
"From 'done' to paid." PayMate logo locks up.

## User flow worth showing
1. Draft: Entering natural language scope into the composer.
2. Prove: The generated invoice appears.
3. Settle: The settlement trace confirms the USDC payment on-chain.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: Twitter launch video with human voiceover explanation
- Interpretation: Confident pacing, premium visuals, and deliberate holds to allow the voiceover and text to breathe. Not chaotic, just smooth.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #f5f3ed (paper) and #171813 (ink)
- Accent: #ff5b2e (orange) and #c9fa78 (lime)
- Text: #171813
- Display font: Trebuchet (mapped from --font-display)
- Body font: NotoSans-Variable (mapped from --font-manrope)
- Strongest visual element: The glowing floating cards and live settlement trace.

## Share copy (draft)
Get paid. Keep the proof. Introducing PayMate: on-chain invoicing with direct USDC settlement and portable reputation on GOAT Network.

## Voiceover script
Voiceover: Meet PayMate. The operating layer for independent work.
Voiceover: Describe the work naturally, and we generate a clean, client-ready invoice in seconds.
Voiceover: Send one link. Clients pay directly from their wallet using USDC on the GOAT network.
Voiceover: Every payment settles instantly and builds your on-chain reputation.
Voiceover: PayMate. From done, to paid.

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse professional accents + voiceover
- Music: subtle ambient warmth (no heavy beat)
- Music treatment: low volume to let the voiceover shine, subtle fade at the end
- Music cue guidance: unavailable / none needed
- Audio-reactive treatment: none (rely on voiceover pacing)
- SFX posture: sparse
- Audio-coupled moments: 
  - Typing the hook
  - Invoice sliding in
- Restraint rule: Do not let SFX or music overpower the voiceover.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Hook — 4s
Text "Get paid. Keep the proof." types out in the center of a dark #171813 screen.
Sequential/interaction: yes — typing character by character
Audio intent: clean mechanical typing sound under the voiceover
Music: subtle ambient drone fades in
Transition mood: clean crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Intelligent Draft — 5s
The "Prompt Card" appears. The text "Brand strategy and launch system..." is typed in. A progress indicator spins briefly, and the resulting invoice slides up from the bottom.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulated typing, then the invoice card slides in
Audio intent: satisfying "success" chime when the invoice appears
Transition mood: smooth slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Settlement Trace — 7s
The "Live Settlement Trace" console appears. The 3 steps light up one by one: "Invoice created", "Transfer submitted", "Settlement verified".
Sequential/interaction: yes — the trace rows light up sequentially
Audio intent: three subtle ticks or pings for each verified step
Transition mood: clean crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Outro — 4s
The PayMate logo lockup appears on a light #f5f3ed background. Text below: "From done to paid."
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: final warm chord, fade out music
Music: swell and fade out
Transition mood: fade to black

**Music mood for this video:** none (or subtle ambient)
**Audio summary:** Focused entirely on clear voiceover, with UI sounds reinforcing the actions on screen.
