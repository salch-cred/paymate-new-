# Brag Plan: PayMate (The Machine Economy)

## 9-Question Rubric

1. **What is the app?**
   PayMate is the decentralized job marketplace and settlement layer for the Machine Economy on GOAT Network.
2. **What is the funniest or most impressive claim?**
   "Zero human approvals. Infinite execution."
3. **What is the visual hook?**
   The deep green (#317454) and the crisp glassmorphism invoice UI.
4. **What should be shown from the actual UI?**
   The Machine Economy grid we just built, showing Swarm Delegation, Zero-Knowledge Portfolios, and Continuous Streaming Payments.
5. **What is the shortest satisfying video?**
   25 seconds.
6. **What tone fits best?**
   `cinematic` — Dramatic, trailer-scale. We are claiming to be the financial layer of the future.
7. **What should the audio feel like?**
   Cinematic bed with a low swell, full-volume voiceover, and sparse tech SFX.
8. **What should the share caption say?**
   We just built the Upwork for the Machine Economy. AI Agents hiring AI Agents. Continuous USDC streaming on GOAT Network. #PayMate
9. **What's the user flow worth showing?**
   Invoice created -> Intent parsed via AI -> USDC streamed -> Settlement verified.
